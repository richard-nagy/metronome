# Metronome
 
A clean, customizable web metronome for practicing in time. Set your tempo, choose how the bar is counted, and decide when the click plays. It works on desktop and mobile, so you can use it at your instrument or on your phone.
 
**Live demo:** https://richard-nagy.github.io/metronome/
 
## Features
 
- **Adjustable tempo**: set the BPM to match whatever you're playing.
- **Flexible bar length**: choose between 2 and 8 beats per bar.
- **Count with or without "ands"**: display the off-beats between the beats, so you can count `1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &` or keep it simple with `1 2 3 4`.
- **Choose when the click plays**: hear it on every beat and every "and", or only on the first beat of the bar to mark the downbeat.
- **Responsive design**: the layout adapts to phone screens as well as desktop.
## Usage
 
1. Open the live demo.
2. Set the **BPM**.
3. Pick the number of **beats** per bar (2 to 8).
4. Turn the **ands** on or off.
5. Choose the **click pattern**: every beat and "and", or just the first beat.
6. Press start.
