# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-13

### Added

- Select and preview five metronome sounds.
- Save named presets containing tempo, rhythm, sound, and display settings; apply or delete them later.
- Persist settings and presets in the browser.
- Enter a replacement tempo by clearing the field, with validation on commit.

### Improved

- Support simple and compound time signatures, subdivisions, count-in, beat patterns, and gradual tempo ramps.
- Validate and normalize saved settings and preset data when loading.
